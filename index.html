Integration By First Principle

Function & Partition Data

Supported: x, numbers, + − * / ^, parentheses, sin, cos, tan, exp, ln, log, sqrt, abs, pi, e. Trigonometric functions use radians.

U must be strictly greater than L.

H = (U − L) / N

Lower Riemann Sum: LN(f) = H Σ mᵢ, where mᵢ = inf f(x) on subinterval i.

Upper Riemann Sum: UN(f) = H Σ Mᵢ, where Mᵢ = sup f(x) on subinterval i.
